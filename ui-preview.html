Gotcha — colors that are NOT variables (they fight the theme)

Dark-gold borders/glows hardcoded as rgba(201,152,42,…) (~25 spots) — these stay dark gold on a light theme and read as brown.
White hairlines rgba(255,255,255,.03–.1) (~13 spots) — invisible on a light bg.
