Gotcha — colors that are NOT variables (they fight the theme)

Dark-gold borders/glows hardcoded as rgba(201,152,42,…) (~25 spots: QA/quiz cards, dividers, glows, focus rings) — these stay dark gold on a light theme and read as brown.
White hairlines rgba(255,255,255,.03–.1) (~13 spots: card borders) — invisible on a light bg, making cards look washed.
Feedback hardcoded emerald #2ecc71 / red #e74c3c + their rgba(…) tints in choice/feedback/blank/build states (only some use var(--green/--red)).
Others: overlay rgba(0,0,0,.65), splash gradient #2a4a1e→#0a1a0e, switch knob #0c1a12, shadows rgba(0,0,0,.35–.5). Browser chrome in manifest.json: background_color #0f1f18, theme_color #1a3a2a.
